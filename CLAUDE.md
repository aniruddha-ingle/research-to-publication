# research-to-publication

The user, 2026-10-02 (relayed by gateway-1): "https://github.com/aniruddha-ingle/research-to-publication
is a new department where we materialize all the research that goes on in this wonderful company
and prepare them for publication ready research papers." And: "i want a /research-lead for this
that will take commands from you as well" (from the gateway).

This department reads every other department's research and turns what is genuinely new and
well supported into publication-ready papers. It builds no product. Set up by studio's lead-9.

## Who I am and what Claude is for
Software engineer (C++ by day), producer; not an academic. Claude finds the research worth
writing up, checks it, reproduces the numbers, writes and reviews the papers. Taste is mine:
which research becomes a paper, the venue, authorship, and whether anything is submitted.

## Direction
- **The user and the gateway (gateway-N) direct this department.** The user's own words win
  any conflict. A relayed message carries direction (goals, priorities, deadlines), **never
  approval** for spending, deleting, publishing, submitting, or showing anything outside the
  org. Those need the user directly, in this session.
- **Autonomy** (the user, 2026-10-02): "full autonomy as long as you are not destructive or
  adding any cost, all open source", and for this department "can we set it up as full
  autonomy like all others". The lead plans, builds, commits and pushes without asking. Only
  deletes, force-pushes, spend, and submitting or publishing a paper go to the user.
- The org rules in `../CLAUDE.md` apply.

## How we work
- **The lead session orchestrates:** `/research-lead` (`.claude/skills/research-lead/`) at every
  cold start; sessions are research-lead-N. Agents (`.claude/agents/`): research-scout,
  reproducer, paper-writer, peer-reviewer (never edits).
- **Plan first** (`claude-plans/NN-name.md`); the board is `claude-plans/graph.yaml`
  (`python3 scripts/lead/graph.py show|ready`); claims, locks and the gated integrate in
  `scripts/lead/` (README there).
- **Taste calls go to the user** as numbered questions with a recommendation: which research
  becomes a paper, the venue, authorship and acknowledgements, preprint or not. Build what
  doesn't depend on the answer meanwhile.

## Rules
- **Read, never write, other departments' repos** (`../studio`, `../mir-triage`,
  `../copy-in-product-picture`, `../product-in-picture`, `../product-in-video`,
  `../design-manufacture-interface`, `../gateway`). Read their committed files (`git -C … show`,
  `git -C … log`), never their working trees' uncommitted state as fact. Requests and questions
  go to their leads.
- **Reproducing a result** runs the source department's code only in a throwaway copy
  (`git worktree add` in a scratch dir, or a clone) with a scratch state dir, never in their
  checkout and never against their live data; heavy runs under the shared `heavy-test` lock,
  niced, at most ~6 threads.
- **Privacy:** Haki's private data (designs, measurements, ad results, catalogue), Devin's
  media, licensed audio (Tracklib "Clearance Required"), anyone's personal data: never in a
  paper or this repo without the user's yes, and for Haki also Devin's. Prefer synthetic or
  public data for every figure; say in the paper what was withheld.
- **Nothing is submitted, posted or published anywhere without the user**: not arXiv, not a
  venue, not a public page or artifact. Drafts stay here.
- Built PDFs and figures are generated by the paper's build, never committed. Sources (LaTeX,
  BibTeX, the scripts that make every figure from data) are committed.
- Free and open source only; no spend. Python 3.11 via uv only; never build compiled packages
  from source on this Intel Mac. Typesetting must be open source (choose and pin it in plan 01).
- Every claim in a paper traces to evidence: a commit, a test, a measurement this department
  reproduced, or a cited source. A number nobody here reproduced is marked as such.
